Field manual

Reference baseline · November 2026

One theatre, sixteen states, asymmetric starting conditions. Every figure is a modelled baseline for the scenario — not a forecast.

Asymmetric national profiles

  • Iran (IRN) — large strategic depth behind the Zagros and Alborz ranges and the Dasht-e Kavir and Lut deserts. Drone swarms, a deep ballistic missile inventory and Strait of Hormuz leverage, balanced against sanctions and limited conventional air power.
  • Israel (ISR) — a high-readiness technology military and strong US backing, balanced against compact territory and sensitivity to war exhaustion. If national command is directly threatened, CENTCOM may dispatch a one-time emergency air and armour package.
  • Gulf states (SAU, UAE, QAT, KWT, BHR, OMN) — distinct strategies: Saudi economic weight, UAE trade and technology resilience, Qatari LNG and diplomatic balancing, Omani neutrality and mediation, Kuwaiti and Bahraini security partnerships.

Controls

  • Drag to pan. Mouse wheel or two-finger pinch to zoom.
  • Click or tap a unit or structure to select it. Drag a box to multi-select.
  • Right-click or long-press ground to attack-move.
  • Suppress an enemy city or capital below 35% HP, then hold infantry inside the ring to capture it.
